## Chapter 9: Central Wholesale Store & Franchise Orders

### 9.1 Central Logistics & Supply Chain
Head Office supplies study kits, standardized printed course textbooks, branded laptop backpacks, and uniform merchandise to affiliated franchises. The **Products Module** (`/super-admin/products`) serves as the central supply-chain hub.

---

### 9.2 Adding & Managing Wholesale Merchandise
1. Navigate to **Products** (`/super-admin/products`).
2. Click **"+ Add Central Product"**.
3. Configure product specifications:
   - **Product Name**: (e.g., *Official ADCA Printed Textbook Bundle — Set of 4 Volumes*).
   - **SKU Code**: Unique inventory code (e.g., *BK-ADCA-SET*).
   - **Category**: *Textbooks*, *Student Kits*, *Apparel*, or *Hardware*.
   - **Wholesale Price to Franchise (₹)**: The discounted rate billed to centers.
   - **Suggested Student Retail Price (₹)**: Maximum retail price recommended for students.
   - **Central Warehouse Stock**: Available units in Head Office warehouse.
   - **Minimum Bulk Order Quantity**: (e.g., *10 Sets*).
4. Upload clear high-resolution product imagery and click **"Save Product"**.

---

### 9.3 Processing & Fulfilling Franchise Orders
When a franchise places a bulk order:
1. An order notification appears in **Products** > **"Franchise Orders"**.
2. Review the order:
   - Requesting Center Name & Center Code.
   - Ordered Items & Quantities.
   - Delivery Address & Center Contact Person.
   - Payment Status (deducted from wallet or paid via central bank transfer).
3. Click **"Process Order"**:
   - Enter **Courier / Logistics Partner Name** (e.g., *Blue Dart*, *Delhivery*, *DTDC*).
   - Enter **Tracking / Consignment Number**.
4. Click **"Mark as Dispatched"**. The franchise receives a dispatch alert with the tracking link.
5. Once delivered, mark the order as **Fulfilled**.
