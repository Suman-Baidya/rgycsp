## Chapter 10: Global Settings, Branding & Central Landing Page

### 10.1 Global Platform Governance
The **Platform Settings Module** (`/super-admin/settings`) controls overarching visual branding, the main national landing page, central legal compliance policies, automated communication relays, and AI engine credentials.

---

### 10.2 Customizing the Main National Landing Page
The main homepage (`https://abcdeduhub.com`) targets prospective franchise partners, corporate affiliations, and nationwide student verification:
1. Navigate to **Settings** (`/super-admin/settings`).
2. **Hero Banner & Value Proposition**:
   - Master Headline (e.g., *India's Premier Multi-Tenant Educational & Skill Training Network*).
   - Supporting tagline highlighting ISO certification, government-recognized certifications, and practical career training.
   - Primary Call-to-Action: *Apply for Franchise Affiliation* (`/franchise`).
   - Secondary Call-to-Action: *Verify Student Certificate* (`/students`).
3. **National Network Statistics Counter**:
   - Configure showcase counters: *500+ Affiliated Centers*, *150,000+ Certified Students*, *100+ Accredited Courses*, *95% Placement Assistance*.
4. **Trust Badges & Affiliations**:
   - Upload accreditation logos, ISO seals, and board recognition certificates.

---

### 10.3 Central Legal Policies & Compliance
ABCD Edu Hub strictly separates Global Legal Policies from local franchise pages:
- **Global Terms of Service**: Governs central platform usage, data privacy, and online portal access.
- **Franchise Affiliation Agreement**: Specifies legal responsibilities, royalty/token terms, examination guidelines, and termination clauses.
- **Privacy Policy**: Complies with national data protection regulations.
- Edit these in **Settings** > **"Legal Policies"** to ensure ongoing regulatory compliance.

---

### 10.4 Communications & System Integrations
1. **Email Delivery (Resend / SMTP)**:
   - Configure central SMTP / Resend API credentials for automated student registration emails, password reset links, and invoice delivery.
2. **Artificial Intelligence Engine (Google Gemini API)**:
   - Enter your enterprise Gemini API Key.
   - This powers the central AI Exam Generator, curriculum assistant, and automated evaluation tools.
3. Click **"Save Global Settings"**. All changes deploy instantly across the network.
