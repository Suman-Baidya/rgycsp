## Chapter 5: Student & Learner Experience Suite

### 5.1 Overview
The **Student Portal** (`/student/dashboard`) provides a consumer-grade, native-feeling digital workspace for every enrolled learner. Accessible on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers, it empowers students to take ownership of their academic progress and credentials.

---

### 5.2 Core Student Features

#### 1. Instant Seamless Sign-In
- **Universal Username**: Students log in using their official **Enrollment Number** (e.g., `WB002-2026-0042`).
- **One-Touch Biometric Sign-In**: Students can bind their smartphone's **Fingerprint** or **Face ID** to log in instantly in under 1 second without typing passwords.

#### 2. Academic Course & Syllabus Tracker
- **Enrolled Course Dossier**: Displays course title, code, duration, batch timing, and assigned faculty.
- **Syllabus Progress Tracker**: Interactive checklist of curriculum modules (e.g., *Module 1: Operating Systems*, *Module 2: Advanced Excel*, *Module 3: Python Basics*) allowing learners to track lesson milestones.

#### 3. Live Attendance Tracker & Contactless QR Pass
- **Daily Attendance Calendar**: Visual calendar displaying daily attendance history with color-coded statuses (*Present*, *Absent*, *Late*, *Excused*).
- **Theory vs Practical Lab Logs**: Track individual attendances for classroom lectures and computer lab sessions.
- **Contactless Smart QR Pass**: Students can present their digital ID card on their phone screen for instant touchless scanning by instructors at the classroom door.
- **Accreditation Eligibility Gauge**: Dynamic percentage gauge warning students if their attendance falls near the 75% threshold required to qualify for examination admit cards.

#### 4. Financial Ledger & Online Fee Payment Portal
- **Transparent Fee Schedule**: Clear breakdown of Total Course Fee, Amount Paid to Date, and Remaining Pending Dues.
- **Installment Dues Schedule**: View upcoming due dates and installment amounts.
- **Online UPI Fee Payments**:
  - Scan the center's official UPI QR code directly on screen.
  - Pay using any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).
  - Submit the 12-digit UTR reference number and upload the payment confirmation screenshot.
- **Automated Digital Receipts**: Once the center approves the transaction, the student can immediately download an official, timestamped **Fee Receipt PDF**.

#### 5. Digital PVC Student ID Card
- **Always Available**: Eliminates lost physical cards; students can display their digital ID card directly on their smartphone screen.
- **Live Verification QR Code**: Anyone scanning the card is securely routed to the official verification page confirming the student's active enrollment.
- **Printable Card Export**: Download high-resolution print files for physical PVC card production.

#### 6. Examination Zone, Online CBT Testing & Admit Cards
- **Instant Admit Card / Hall Ticket Download**:
  - Download official examination Admit Cards directly from the student dashboard.
  - Displays student photo, enrollment number, assigned examination center, date/time, and entry rules.
- **Interactive Online CBT Examinations**:
  - Take scheduled tests directly on smartphone or computer with real-time countdown timers.
  - Instant automated grading for objective assessments.
  - Visual **Radar Performance Charts** and **Score Trend Analytics** evaluating subject strengths (Core Concepts, Problem Solving, Execution, Speed).
- **Online Scorecards & Marksheets**: View module-wise theoretical and practical marks, aggregate percentages, and awarded grades.
- **Accredited Certificate Download**: Students who pass their curriculum can preview and download their official digital diploma complete with central board seals and verification QR codes.

#### 7. Profile & Security Self-Service
- Update personal contact information and emergency phone numbers.
- Self-service password changes.
- Multi-device Passkey management (registering phone, tablet, and PC).
