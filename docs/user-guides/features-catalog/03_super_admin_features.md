## Chapter 3: Central Board & Super Admin Suite

### 3.1 Overview
The **Super Administrator Command Center** (`/super-admin`) is tailored for central educational trusts, national councils, and corporate franchisors to supervise hundreds of branches from a unified executive console.

---

### 3.2 Core Super Admin Capabilities

#### 1. Franchise Center Management & Provisioning
- **Online Affiliation Intake**: Review prospective franchise applications submitted via the public portal. Inspect center photos, infrastructure specifications, and director credentials before approval.
- **Manual Center Setup**: Instantly provision an accredited study center:
  - Set official Center Code (e.g., `WB-002`) which is permanently embedded into student enrollment numbers.
  - Assign custom Subdomain slug (e.g., `wb-002.abcdeduhub.com`).
  - Set Director credentials and allocate welcome token credits.
  - Set **Document Authority**: Grant autonomous certification privileges or require central Head Office approval for every student certificate.
- **One-Click Direct Portal Access ("Jump to Franchise")**: Master administrators can enter any franchise's internal dashboard with a single click to troubleshoot, audit, or assist without needing local passwords.
- **Bulk CSV Onboarding**: Import hundreds of legacy centers simultaneously using standardized CSV templates.

#### 2. Global Student Registry & Central Verification
- **Cross-Center Student Search**: Search learners across the entire national network by Enrollment Number, Name, Phone Number, Course, or State.
- **Accredited Registration Card Verification**: Centralized review of student details and passport photographs. Approving an application unlocks the official printable Registration Card with the central board seal.
- **Certificate Fraud Prevention**: Direct oversight of examination scores and attendance records prior to issuing accredited diplomas.

#### 3. Accredited Global Course Master
- **Standardized Curriculum Governance**: Super Admin authors accredited courses (e.g., *ADCA*, *DCA*, *Tally Prime*, *Python*), complete with structured module topics, practical hours, and recommended fee schedules.
- **Course Group Taxonomy**: Group programs into recognized categories (*Diplomas*, *Accounting*, *Vocational*, *Kids Computer*).
- **Network-Wide Propagation**: Changes made at Head Office instantly reflect across all affiliated centers without manual data entry.

#### 4. Token Economy & Merchant Banking Operations
- **Pre-Paid Operational Currency**: Central services (AI exam paper generation, PVC ID card issuance, tamper-proof certificates) are billed via internal wallet tokens.
- **Central Payment Setup**: Configure Head Office UPI ID, Dynamic Merchant QR Codes, and official bank account details.
- **Recharge Request Reconciliation**: Franchise owners submit top-up requests with uploaded UPI/Bank transfer payment slips. Head Office verifies the UTR reference and approves requests with **one-click token minting**.
- **Direct Wallet Adjustments**: Credit or debit franchise tokens manually with mandatory audit remarks.

#### 5. Visual Document Designer & Professional Printing Layouts
- **Visual Template Builder**: Design standardized credentials for the entire network: Certificates of Excellence, Academic Marksheets, Student PVC ID Cards, and **Examination Admit Cards (Hall Tickets)**.
- **Dynamic Variable Tokens**: Placeholders like `{student_name}`, `{enrollment_no}`, `{center_name}`, `{course_title}`, `{grade}`, `{exam_center}`, `{seat_no}`, and `{issue_date}` automatically resolve upon generation.
- **Dual-Mode Industrial Print Layout Engine**:
  - **Single Page Mode (Standard Desktop)**: Generates 1 document per page with exact dimensions and orientation (portrait ID cards or landscape certificates) for standard desktop laser and PVC card printers.
  - **Double Page / Press Print Mode (18x12 Inch Commercial Offset Layout)**: Renders two documents per page positioned side-by-side with calibrated margins on standard 18x12 inch offset press sheets. This allows institutions and commercial printing presses to print and cut certificates, marksheets, or duplex ID cards in high volume with zero waste.
- **Dynamic Anti-Counterfeit QR Code**: Embeds an encrypted QR code linking directly to the central online verification portal (`/verify/certificate/{id}`).
- **PVC ID Card Formats**: Formats dual-sided CR80 standard ID cards ready for direct PVC thermal card printing.

#### 6. Regional State Manager Network
- **Territorial Jurisdiction**: Appoint regional educational coordinators for specific States and Districts.
- **Regional Performance Dashboards**: Track center growth, student intake quotas, and compliance inspection reports across geographical territories.

#### 7. Central Wholesale Store & Merchandise Supply Chain
- **Wholesale Inventory Management**: Supply accredited printed textbooks, branded student laptop backpacks, uniform t-shirts, and computer hardware kits.
- **Bulk Order Fulfillment**: Review purchase orders from franchises, record logistics consignment numbers (Blue Dart, Delhivery, DTDC), and track delivery statuses.

#### 8. National Platform Governance & System Logs
- **Main Landing Page Builder**: Customize `https://abcdeduhub.com` with value propositions, trust badges, ISO certifications, and affiliation inquiry forms.
- **Immutable System Activity Logs**: Complete audit trail capturing every administrative action (who approved a center, who minted tokens, when, and from which IP address) with one-click CSV export.
