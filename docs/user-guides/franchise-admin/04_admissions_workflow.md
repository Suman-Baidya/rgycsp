## Chapter 4: Admissions Processing & Workflow

### 4.1 The Admissions Inbox
When prospective students submit an online application via your website, it lands in your center's **Admissions Inbox**. A pulsing red notification badge on the sidebar indicates unreviewed applications waiting for administrative decision.

---

### 4.2 Reviewing Incoming Applications

#### Step-by-Step Review Workflow:
1. In the sidebar, click **Admissions** (`/admin/admissions`) or go to **Students** and select the **"Admissions"** tab.
2. The **Online Admission Applications** table displays:
   - **Student Name & Photo**
   - **Applied Course**
   - **Contact (Phone & Email)**
   - **Submission Date & Time**
   - **Current Status** (`PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`)
3. Click on any application row or click the **"Review Application"** eye icon to open the full applicant dossier.

#### Inside the Application Dossier:
The review modal presents comprehensive applicant information:
- **Personal Details**: Full Name, Father's/Mother's Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Blood Group, Category.
- **Contact & Address**: Permanent address, WhatsApp number, emergency contact.
- **Academic Qualifications**: Previous school/college, marks/percentage, board.
- **Custom Question Responses**: Answers to any custom dynamic questions configured in your admission settings.
- **Uploaded Document Verification**:
  - Click to preview the student's uploaded passport photo.
  - Click to inspect uploaded Government ID cards (Aadhaar/Voter ID) and educational marksheets.
  - Check image clarity and verify that the uploaded photo is appropriate for the student's official ID Card and Certificate.

---

### 4.3 Approving an Application & Enrolling the Student

When the student's documents and admission fee payment are verified:

1. In the application review window, click the green **"Approve Application"** button.
2. An **Enrollment Modal** will open prompting you for initial academic placement:
   - **Assign Batch**: Select an existing batch from the dropdown (e.g., *Batch 2026-A (Morning 9 AM)*) or create a new batch.
   - **Admission Date**: Confirm the official joining date (defaults to today).
   - **Roll Number / Serial (Optional)**: Set an internal classroom roll number if needed.
3. Click **"Confirm & Finalize Admission"**.

#### What Happens Automatically Upon Approval:
- **Enrollment Number Generation**: The system automatically allocates a unique, accredited student enrollment number based on your center code and the admission year (e.g., `WB002-2026-0042`).
- **Student User Account Creation**: A secure student portal user account is automatically provisioned using the student's enrollment number as their username.
- **Student Profile Creation**: The applicant record is promoted to the **Active Students Directory**.
- **Financial Ledger Creation**: The student is linked to the course fee schedule, with initial fee installments scheduled in the **Fees Management** ledger.
- **ID Card & Certificate Ready**: The student's photo and verified credentials are immediately linked to the ID Card and Certificate generator.

---

### 4.4 Rejecting an Application
If an applicant uploaded fraudulent documents, duplicate submissions, or does not meet eligibility criteria:
1. In the application review window, click **"Reject Application"**.
2. Select or enter the **Rejection Reason** (e.g., *Unclear ID proof uploaded*, *Ineligible qualification for advanced course*, or *Duplicate submission*).
3. Confirm the rejection.
4. The application status changes to `REJECTED`, and the record is archived. The applicant cannot access the student portal.

---

### 4.5 Downloading the Official Admission Application PDF
To maintain offline physical records or obtain student/parent physical signatures:
1. Click the **"Download Admission PDF"** button on the application record.
2. The system generates a clean, printable PDF containing:
   - Center name, logo, center code, and official affiliation headers.
   - Student's passport photograph and signature box.
   - All submitted biographical and academic details.
   - Course name, duration, and admission fee breakdown.
   - The institute's customized **Admission Declaration** text.
   - Student signature line, parent/guardian signature line, and authorized center seal/signature box.
3. Print or archive this PDF in your physical student enrollment binder.
