## Chapter 7: Attendance Tracking System

### 7.1 Overview
Regular class attendance is mandatory for student progress and certification eligibility. The **Attendance Module** (`/admin/attendance`) provides a rapid, one-click daily register for both teachers and administrators.

---

### 7.2 Taking Daily Batch Attendance

#### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Navigate to **Attendance** (`/admin/attendance`) in the sidebar.
2. At the top of the register, select:
   - **Batch**: Choose the specific cohort/batch (e.g., *Morning ADCA Batch 9:00 AM*).
   - **Date**: Select the attendance date (defaults to today's date).
3. The register populates with all students currently enrolled in that batch.
4. For each student, choose one of four attendance statuses:
   - **Present (`P`)** [Green]: Student attended the class session.
   - **Absent (`A`)** [Red]: Student did not attend without prior notice.
   - **Late (`L`)** [Amber]: Student arrived after the scheduled session start time.
   - **Excused (`E`)** [Blue]: Student was granted prior leave (medical, personal).

#### Productivity Shortcuts:
- **"Mark All Present"**: Click this button at the top right to instantly mark every student in the batch as *Present*. You can then quickly toggle only the few students who are absent or late, saving significant administrative time.
- **Save Register**: Click **"Submit Attendance"** to finalize the records for the selected date.

---

### 7.3 Attendance Reports & Eligibility Thresholds
1. Switch to the **"Attendance Reports"** tab.
2. Select a Date Range (e.g., *Current Month* or *Entire Semester*).
3. The report displays:
   - Total Sessions Held.
   - Total Days Present / Absent for each student.
   - **Overall Attendance Percentage (%)**.
4. Students falling below the minimum accreditation threshold (e.g., less than 75% attendance) are highlighted in red to alert staff prior to approving examination admit cards.
