## Chapter 8: Exam Zone & Question Paper Generator

### 8.1 The Exam Management Module
The **Exam Zone** (`/admin/exam-generator`) allows franchise administrators and faculty to create standardized examinations, manage question banks, and export ready-to-print assessment papers complete with your center's header and instructions.

---

### 8.2 Creating a New Examination Paper

#### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Navigate to **Exam Zone** (`/admin/exam-generator`) from the sidebar.
2. Click **"+ Create New Exam"**.
3. Fill in the primary assessment parameters:
   - **Exam Title**: Enter a descriptive name (e.g., *ADCA Semester 1 Final Assessment*).
   - **Course**: Select the target course from your catalog.
   - **Duration**: Specify the exam time in minutes (e.g., `90 Minutes`).
   - **Total Marks**: Overall score (e.g., `100 Marks`).
   - **Passing Threshold**: Minimum marks required to pass (e.g., `40%`).

---

### 8.3 Building the Question Paper
You can build the paper using custom questions:
1. Click **"+ Add Question"**.
2. Select the **Question Type**:
   - **Multiple Choice (MCQ)**: Enter the question stem, add 4 options (`A`, `B`, `C`, `D`), and check the radio button for the correct key.
   - **True / False**: Enter the statement and designate whether True or False is correct.
   - **Short Answer / Practical**: Enter descriptive or coding/practical questions and assign mark weightage (e.g., *5 Marks*).
3. Assign marks to each question until the sum matches the Total Marks target.

---

### 8.4 Generating & Printing Question Papers
1. Click **"Preview Paper"** to inspect the layout.
2. The system formats the document into a professional academic test paper containing:
   - Institute name, Center Code, and Logo in the header.
   - Student Name, Roll Number, and Date fill-in blanks.
   - Standard Examination Rules and Instructions.
   - Two-column or one-column question formatting.
3. Click **"Print / Download PDF"** to print physical copies for classroom paper-based exams.
