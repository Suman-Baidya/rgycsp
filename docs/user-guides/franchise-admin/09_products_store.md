## Chapter 9: Products & Institute Store

### 9.1 Overview
The **Products & Store Module** (`/admin/products`) enables centers to offer physical study materials, branded merchandise, and supplies directly to students:
- Comprehensive Course Textbooks and Modules
- Institute Branded Laptop Bags & Backpacks
- Student Uniforms / T-Shirts
- Computer Hardware Kits & Components (e.g., Arduino/Raspberry Pi kits for hardware courses)

---

### 9.2 Adding & Managing Store Items

#### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Navigate to **Products & Store** (`/admin/products`).
2. Click **"+ Add Product"**.
3. Fill in the product configuration:
   - **Product Name**: (e.g., *Official ADCA Study Module (Volumes 1 & 2)*).
   - **SKU / Item Code**: Optional internal tracking code (e.g., *BOOK-ADCA-01*).
   - **Category**: (e.g., *Books*, *Apparel*, *Hardware Kit*).
   - **Selling Price (₹)**: Amount charged to the student.
   - **Cost Price (₹)**: Optional wholesale cost for margin tracking.
   - **Stock Quantity**: Current units available at your center inventory.
   - **Product Image**: Upload a clear photograph of the product.
   - **Status (Active/Inactive)**: Toggle whether the item appears in the student store.
4. Click **"Save Product"**.

---

### 9.3 Fulfilling Student Store Orders
When students request or purchase items via their student portal:
1. In the Products dashboard, click the **"Orders"** tab.
2. Review pending orders, verify payment collection, and click **"Mark as Fulfilled / Handed Over"**.
3. The inventory stock count automatically decrements by the fulfilled quantity.
