## Chapter 11: Public Website & Center Branding Customization

### 11.1 Your Dedicated Public Website
Every franchise center receives a dedicated, professional public website hosted on your custom subdomain (e.g., `https://wb-002.abcdeduhub.com` or `/app/wb-002`). This website serves as your 24/7 digital front door, attracting prospective students, displaying your course catalog, publishing notice board circulars, and accepting online admissions.

---

### 11.2 Accessing the Website Builder Settings
1. Navigate to **Landing Page** (`/admin/settings`) in the sidebar.
2. The settings panel is organized into clean, focused tabs:
   - **General & Branding**
   - **Hero Banner**
   - **About Section**
   - **Notice Board**
   - **Photo Gallery**
   - **Contact & Location**
   - **Social Links**

---

### 11.3 Step-by-Step Customization

#### 1. General Branding & Identity
- **Institute Name**: Your official franchise display name (e.g., *ABCD Computer Academy - Kolkata Central*).
- **Institute Tagline**: A concise marketing motto (e.g., *Empowering Digital Careers Since 2018*).
- **Center Logo**: Upload your official high-resolution logo (PNG or SVG format with transparent background recommended).
- **Favicon**: Upload a 32x32 pixel icon that appears in browser tabs.
- **Certificate Watermark**: Upload a subtle monochrome seal or crest that appears behind student certificates.

#### 2. Hero Banner (The First Impression)
- **Headline**: The large, bold headline visitors see when landing on your site (e.g., *Launch Your Career in IT & Professional Accounting*).
- **Sub-headline**: A compelling supporting statement highlighting your placement record, certified faculty, and practical labs.
- **Call-to-Action (CTA) Button 1**: Configure the primary button text (e.g., *Apply for Admission*) and link destination (`/admission`).
- **Call-to-Action (CTA) Button 2**: Configure a secondary button (e.g., *View Courses* or *Chat on WhatsApp*).
- **Hero Background Image**: Upload high-resolution photographs of your students working in the computer lab.

#### 3. About Us Section
- Share your center's founding story, infrastructure highlights, state-of-the-art computer labs, AC classrooms, and experienced mentors.
- Highlight key metrics: *Students Trained (e.g., 2,500+)*, *Placement Rate (e.g., 94%)*, and *Years of Excellence*.

#### 4. Notice Board & Announcements
Keep students and parents informed of important dates:
1. Click the **Notice Board** tab.
2. Click **"+ Add Notice"**.
3. Enter the **Notice Title** (e.g., *Holiday Notice: Center Closed for Diwali*, or *ADCA Term-End Exam Schedule Announced*).
4. Enter the detailed circular text.
5. Set an optional **Expiration Date** so past notices automatically unpublish.
6. Check **"Mark as Urgent / Flashing"** to display an attention-grabbing ticker at the top of your website.

#### 5. Photo Gallery & Campus Tour
Prospective students want to see your physical center before visiting:
1. Go to the **Gallery** tab.
2. Click **"Upload Photos"**.
3. Select clear images of your:
   - Modern computer lab setups and workstations.
   - High-tech theory classrooms and projectors.
   - Student award ceremonies, annual fests, and certificate distribution days.
4. Add descriptive captions to each photo.

#### 6. Contact Information & Google Maps Embed
Ensure prospective learners can easily visit or call your center:
- **Physical Address**: Complete street address, landmark, city, state, and pin code.
- **Phone Numbers**: Primary reception desk phone and admissions helpline.
- **WhatsApp Contact**: Direct WhatsApp chat number.
- **Google Maps Embed Link**: Paste your Google Maps `iframe` link so an interactive map appears on your contact page.

#### 7. Social Media Links
Link your active social profiles:
- Facebook Page URL
- Instagram Profile URL
- YouTube Channel URL
- LinkedIn Profile URL

#### 8. Saving & Publishing
1. Click **"Save Settings & Publish"** at the bottom of the page.
2. Your public website updates instantly without needing server restarts or developer assistance.
